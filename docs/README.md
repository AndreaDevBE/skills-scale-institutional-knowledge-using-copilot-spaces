# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation. This collection of guides provides a lightweight, consistent approach to planning, delivering, and improving projects across cross-functional teams. The goal is to help teams move from idea to execution with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and shared visibility into progress, risks, and dependencies. These documents are meant to serve as a practical knowledge base for onboarding, execution, and continuous improvement.

OctoAcme’s project management approach is grounded in a customer-first, iterative delivery model. Work begins with initiation, where the team validates the problem, aligns stakeholders, defines success metrics, and decides whether to proceed. Once approved, the team moves into planning, breaking work into manageable increments with backlog priorities, milestones, acceptance criteria, and Definition of Done. Execution follows a defined rhythm of daily standups, weekly updates, demos, and progress tracking, while quality and risk management are embedded throughout the lifecycle. This ensures the team stays coordinated, focused, and transparent while delivering value incrementally.

The lifecycle continues through release and deployment, where teams verify readiness, run smoke tests, communicate updates, and prepare rollback plans for critical issues. At the end of each sprint or milestone, retrospectives capture what went well, what could improve, and what actions will be taken next. Across all stages, OctoAcme emphasizes clear roles, regular communication, and evidence-based decision-making. By combining structured processes with strong stakeholder communication and quality assurance practices, the team can reduce delivery risk and maintain a repeatable path from concept to outcome.

## Project Lifecycle Summary

OctoAcme’s work follows a five-phase lifecycle:

1. Initiation — Validate the business need, align stakeholders, and define measurable outcomes
2. Planning — Break scope into backlog items, estimate work, confirm dependencies, and set milestones
3. Execution — Deliver in iterations with standups, tracking, regular reviews, and issue escalation
4. Release — Validate quality, deploy with safeguards, and communicate outcomes to stakeholders
5. Close & Retrospective — Review results, capture learnings, and turn improvements into follow-up actions

## Core Roles

- Project Manager (PM): Coordinates planning, execution, risk management, and communication
- Product Manager / Product Lead: Defines desired outcomes, priorities, and success metrics
- Developers: Build features, write tests, and deliver reliable software
- QA / Testing: Validate acceptance criteria and quality before release
